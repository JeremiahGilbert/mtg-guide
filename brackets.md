# Commander Brackets

Commander brackets are a system developed by WOTC to allow a general comparison between the relative power of commander decks.
Commander decks can be rougly divided into 5 brackets, with bracket 1 being the weakest and bracket 5 being the most powerful.
In general, decks can play with a bracket above or below without too much issue, but larger bracket differences can lead to large power disparity between decks and unfun gameplay.

## Bracket 1

Bracket 1 commander decks are decks built with winning as a secondary concern.
For example, a deck that features bears in the art of each card prioritizes aesthetics over its ability to win.
These decks are closer to interesting art pieces or card collections than functional decks.

Bracket 1 decks contain:
- NO game changers
- NO mass land denial
- NO extra turns
- NO 2-card combos

## Bracket 2

Bracket 2 decks are decks made with the goal of winning in mind, but are slower, incremental, and telegraphed.
These decks are usually grindy midrange decks that accrue value over the course of the game.
They are primarily proactive rather than reactive, focusing on advancing their own gameplan than attempting to stop opponents from advancing theirs.
Decks in bracket 2 may contain cards that are suboptimal mechanically, but are included for fun or to showcase an obscure combo.
Bracket 2 decks are definitionally on par with the average modern commander preconstructed deck.

Bracket 2 decks contain:
- NO game changers
- NO mass land denial
- NO chaining extra turns (individual extra turns allowed)
- NO 2-card combos

## Bracket 3

Bracket 3 decks are decks that have a strong, focused gameplan, with high quality cards and synergies to accomplish it.
They mix proactivity with reactivity, forwarding their own gameplan, but preventing opponents from advancing theirs as necessary.
While the card quality is higher in bracket 3, cards are still selected based on an overall theme over always taking the strictly optimal cards.

Bracket 3 decks contain:
- UP TO 3 game changers
- NO mass land denial
- NO chaining extra turns
- NO early 2-card combos

## Bracket 4

Bracket 4 decks are made with the sole focus of winning with no restrictions.
They are generally the strongest decks that a particular commander allows, and are as quick to win and lethal as possible.
Bracket 4 decks are built for maximum efficiency and card quality.

There are no deck restrictions in bracket 4.

## Bracket 5

Bracket 5 decks are similar to bracket 4, except they also adhere to the [cEDH metagame](https://cedh-decklist-database.com/).

There are no deck restrictions in bracket 5.
