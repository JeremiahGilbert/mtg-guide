# Commander Deck Building

## General Deck Structure

| Category       | Quantity | Description                               |
|----------------|----------|-------------------------------------------|
| Commander      | 1        | The basis of the deck                     |
| Lands          | ~36      | Do not skimp on lands                     |
| Ramp           | ~10      | Mana rocks, mana dorks, and ramp spells   |
| Card Advantage | ~10      | Draw, scry, surveil, and impulse effects  |
| Interaction    | ~10      | Removal, Protection, Counters             |
| Board Wipes    | ~3       | Mass Destruction, Mass Exile, Mass Bounce |
| Theme          | ~30      | Commander synergy and win conditions      |

### Always try to match your theme

The Lands, Ramp, Card Advantage, and Interaction sections don't all need to be generic value pieces.
Prioritize cards that fulfill those functions while also working with the theme of the deck.
These alternatives may not be as mana efficient, but the extra cost is offset by advancing your strategy.

### These numbers are flexible

Depending on what your deck does and at what speed, you may need to adjust these numbers.
An artifact deck might drop to 34 lands and use more mana rocks.
A grindy midrange landfall deck may push up to 40+ lands and 14 ramp spells.
A glacial control deck might lean into more stax and interaction to keep everyone else at their pace.
These numbers only serve as a rough estimate.
The one exception to this is cutting lands to make room for more "real" cards.
If you feel that you're not seeing enough good cards, that usually means you need to increase the quality of your nonland cards and/or your card advantage.

## Win Conditions

Decide how your deck wins.
Try to have at least one primary win condition and a secondary backup condition.
Every card in your deck should either work towards one of your own win conditions, or towards disrupting an opponents win condition.

Some common win conditions include:
- Combat damage: Play big creatures and hit your opponents until they stop moving
- Tokens: Create an army of token creatures, then use anthems and overrun effects to overwhelm your opponents
- Voltron: Stack up one creature (usually the commander) with buffs and evasion and kill with lethal (commander) damage
- Combo: Assemble a collection of pieces that produce extreme/infinite value
- Life Drain: Slowly bleed out opponents with small, recurring damage
- Mill: Completely empty opponents libraries, causing them to lose when they next attempt to draw

## Lands

Lands in commander can be broken down into 3 types.
These are, in order of importance, mana fixing lands, utility lands, and basic lands.
Ideally, the majority of a decks landbase will be mana fixing lands, with a few utility lands as consistency will allow.
The remaining slots are filled with basics to provide targets for ramp spells and fetchlands.
Roughly 8-12 lands should be basics at most.

The land cycles in order of inclusion priority are listed below:
1. Fetchlands
2. ABUR Duals
3. Shocklands
4. Tricycle Lands
5. Surveil Lands
6. Bondlands
7. Fast Lands
8. Check Lands
9. Tango Lands
10. Slowland
11. Painlands
12. Pathways
13. Bounce Lands
14. Verges
15. Filter Lands
16. Signet Lands
17. Temples
18. Pinglands
19. Bridges
20. Snow Duals
21. DMU Duals
22. Guildgates
