# Protection

Decks that rely heavily on their commander will want to keep them around at all costs, while cecks that create a threataningly large board presence will want to preserve their board as best they can.
If a deck only needs to protect its commander, it is more appropriate to include single target protection; If a deck needs to protect their entire board against a board wipe, more expensive, wider reaching protection is appropriate.
Protection can come in the form of giving one or more creatures or permanents indestructible, hexproof, shroud, protection, damage prevention, or phasing them out.
Different colors will have different methods of giving protection.

## Protection in White

### The Mother, The Daughter, and The Weird Dog

In decks that both need to strongly protect their commander and have access to white, there are three very powerful protection granting creatures:

1. Mother of Runes
2. Giver of Runes
3. Skrelv, Defector Mite

These creatures all have a tap ability to grant any creature protection to a color for a turn.
In decks that want protection for their commander that can run them almost certainly should.

### Teferi's Protection

Arguably the single best protection spell in all of Magic, Teferi's protection prevents all interaction with you and your permanents until your next turn.
A strong consideration for any deck that needs mass board protection.
